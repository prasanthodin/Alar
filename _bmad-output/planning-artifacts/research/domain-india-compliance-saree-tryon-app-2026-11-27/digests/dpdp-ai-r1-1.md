## Obligations the product would have to build

Timing note: per C3-C5 most DPDP duties are not yet legally in force on 2026-11-27 and commence about 13 May 2027 unless MeitY shortens the period (C44). Until then the IT Act s.43A / SPDI regime reportedly still applies (C9, C10).

1. Standalone, plain-language, itemised consent notice shown before photo upload: lists the photo (and any other data) and the try-on purpose; links to withdraw, exercise rights, and complain to the Board. (C12, C13) - **clear**
2. Affirmative, purpose-specific consent for the photo, not bundled with other purposes, limited to data necessary for try-on. (C11) - **clear**. Whether s.7(a) "voluntary provision" could be used instead of consent - **needs lawyer** (C32).
3. Consent request available in English or any Eighth Schedule language at the user's option. (C14) - **clear** on the duty; how many languages must actually be offered - **needs lawyer**.
4. Withdrawal as easy as giving consent; on withdrawal erase the photo and generated images and make the processor(s) erase. (C15, C16) - **clear**
5. Purpose limitation: do not reuse photos for model training, ads or analytics without separate consent. (C11) - **clear** as principle; specific reuse cases **needs lawyer**.
6. Retention: delete photos once the try-on purpose is served (C16); the three-year fixed rule only applies at 2 crore+ registered users (C17). But rule 8(3) / 6(1)(e) require one-year retention of "personal data, associated traffic data and other logs" (C18). How these combine for an uploaded photo - **unsettled / needs lawyer**.
7. Security safeguards: encryption, access control, access logs with one-year retention, backups, and a security clause in every processor contract. (C19) - **clear**
8. Breach process: notify every affected user without delay and the Board without delay plus a detailed report within 72 hours. (C20) - **clear** (practical filing route depends on Board becoming functional, C6).
9. Published contact person for data questions; grievance mechanism with a published response time of 90 days or less; published means to make rights requests. (C21) - **clear**
10. Rights handling: access summary incl. list of processors the photo was shared with (so the app must be able to name the AI provider/aggregator), correction, erasure, nomination. (C22) - **clear**
11. Children (under 18): either keep under-18s out or obtain verifiable parental consent via rule 10 methods before processing their photo; no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads at under-18s; no exemption for retail. (C23, C24, C25) - duty **clear**; what age-assurance is sufficient when the user does not declare being a child, and whether self-declared age suffices - **unsettled / needs lawyer**. Processing done only to confirm a user is not a child is exempted from ss.9(1) and 9(3) (C25).
12. Cross-border: transfers to a foreign AI model host are permitted unless the destination is notified as restricted; none reported notified. Build a configurable region/provider switch and track notifications. (C26, C27) - **clear** on the rule; current list status rests on secondary sources.
13. Processor chain: a "valid contract" with each processor; the shop remains fully responsible for what the AI provider and any aggregator do; contract must cover security and erasure. Whether an API aggregator's standard terms amount to a sufficient contract, and whether aggregator and model host are both the shop's processors or separate fiduciaries - **needs lawyer**. (C28, C29)
14. Someone else's photo: the personal/domestic exemption protects the uploading individual, not the shop; the Act requires consent of the person the data relates to and has no express mechanism for user-supplied third-party data. Product measures (attestation that the photo is of the user or used with permission, takedown route for depicted persons) are derived mitigations, not text-mandated. (C31, C32) - **unsettled / needs lawyer**
15. Block generation of unlawful synthetic images (nudity, sexually explicit, privacy-invasive, deceptive depictions of a real person). If the shop is an "intermediary", this is an express rule 3(3)(a)(i) duty; in any case the IT Act/BNS offences listed by the Government apply to such content. (C33, C36) - content prohibition **clear**; rule 3(3) applicability **unsettled**.
16. Visible "AI-generated" label on each try-on image and embedded provenance metadata with a unique identifier, not removable. Mandatory only if the shop is an "intermediary" offering an SGI tool. (C35, C36, C38) - **unsettled / needs lawyer**. A pending draft would require the label to be continuous and clearly visible (C41).
17. User SGI declaration flow: applies only to significant social media intermediaries; not required of a shop on the text read. (C37) - **clear** (not applicable), provided the app adds no social/sharing feed.
18. Until about 13 May 2027: confirm what SPDI Rules / s.43A currently require for photos (privacy policy, consent, reasonable security practices). (C9, C10) - **needs lawyer**
19. Monitor: commencement compression (C44), Board appointments (C6), s.16 notifications (C27), SDF notifications (C46), IT Rules draft (C41), MeitY advisories (C42). - **clear** as a process need.

## Glossary

- **Data Fiduciary** - "any person who alone or in conjunction with other persons determines the purpose and means of processing of personal data" (Act s.2(i), S2). The shop.
- **Data Principal** - "the individual to whom the personal data relates"; for a child includes the parents or lawful guardian (s.2(j), S2). The person in the photo.
- **Data Processor** - "any person who processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary" (s.2(k), S2). The AI model host and, probably, the aggregator.
- **Personal data** - "any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data" (s.2(t), S2).
- **Child** - "an individual who has not completed the age of eighteen years" (s.2(f), S2).
- **Verifiable consent** - consent as specified in rule 10 (parent of a child) or rule 11 (guardian of a person with disability) (rule 2(1)(d), S1).
- **Consent Manager** - "a person registered with the Board, who acts as a single point of contact to enable a Data Principal to give, manage, review and withdraw her consent through an accessible, transparent and interoperable platform" (s.2(g), S2); must be an Indian company (S3, S4).
- **Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF)** - a Data Fiduciary or class notified by the Central Government based on factors including volume and sensitivity of data and risk to Data Principals (s.10(1), S2); extra duties: DPO in India, annual DPIA and audit (s.10(2), rule 13, S1).
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** - individual appointed by an SDF, based in India, responsible to the board of directors (s.10(2)(a), S2). Non-SDFs need only a contact person (rule 9, S1).
- **Data Protection Board of India (the Board)** - adjudicatory body that inquires into breaches and imposes penalties (s.33, S2; S3); appeals go to TDSAT (S3).
- **Personal data breach** - "any unauthorised processing of personal data or accidental ..." (s.2(u), S2, definition only partly displayed in this run).
- **Legitimate uses** - grounds in s.7 for processing without consent, including data voluntarily provided for a specified purpose (s.7(a), S2).
- **Specified purpose** - the purpose stated in the notice; consent is limited to it (ss.5, 6(1), S2).
- **User account** - the online account registered by the Data Principal with the Data Fiduciary, including profiles, email address, mobile number (rule 2(1)(c), S1).
- **E-commerce entity** (for the retention schedule) - person who owns, operates or manages a digital facility or platform for e-commerce as defined in the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, excluding a marketplace seller (Third Schedule note, S1).
- **Synthetically generated information (SGI)** - see C35 (IT Rules rule 2(1)(wa), S5).
- **Intermediary / social media intermediary / significant social media intermediary (SSMI)** - "social media intermediary" is an intermediary which "primarily or solely enables online interaction between two or more users" (rule 2(1)(w), S5); SSMI is one above the notified user threshold (rule 2(1)(v), S5; 50 lakh per PIB S7). "Intermediary" itself is defined in IT Act s.2(1)(w) - not retrieved in this run.
- **SPDI Rules** - the 2011 "sensitive personal data or information" rules under IT Act s.43A; reported still in force until s.43A is omitted (C9). Text not retrieved.

## Contradictions

1. **13 vs 14 November 2025 (and so 13 vs 14 Nov 2026 / May 2027).** The Gazette is dated 13 Nov 2025 (S1) but carries e-publication ID "14112025"; PIB says the Rules were "notified on 14 November 2025" (S3). Commencement runs from "the date of publication". Secondary sources mostly compute 13 May 2027; at least one uses 14 May. Unresolved; a one-day difference.
2. **Is the Board functioning?** PIB describes the Board as established and digital-first (S3, S4); LiveLaw and trackers report no Chairperson or Members appointed as of Nov 2026; other sites call it operational (S9). No official appointment notification was found.
3. **Erase-when-done vs keep-for-a-year.** s.8(7) requires erasure when the purpose is served or consent is withdrawn "unless retention is necessary for compliance with any law" (C16); rule 8(3) requires keeping "such personal data, associated traffic data and other logs" for at least one year and its illustration says this holds "even if X deletes her account" (C18). Whether rule 8(3) compels a shop to keep an uploaded photo for a year, or only transaction records and logs, is not resolved by the text.
4. **SPDI status.** Earlier commentary said SPDI Rules lapsed on notification of the DPDP Rules; later sources tie repeal to s.44(2) commencing about 13 May 2027 (S11). The later view is consistent with the reported tranche structure (C4).
5. **Scope of the proposed 12-month compression.** Some reports say it targets only SDFs; others quote MeitY as proposing it generally; sources also disagree on which date would move (S10).
6. **PIB vs rule text on SGI.** PIB (S7) describes deepfakes as including "synthetic audio, video and text"; the notified SGI definition covers only audio, visual, audio-visual (S5). The October 2026 advisory reportedly widens labelling beyond the rule (S12).
