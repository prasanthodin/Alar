## Requirements the app and admin must meet

Store-facing
1. In-app "report this image" control on every generated try-on result, reachable without leaving the app, feeding a moderation queue the admin can see. [P1, P2, P3; A1] Flag: clear for Play; interpretation for Apple (A2).
2. Prevent sexual, nude or "undress"-style output: fixed, garment-only prompts; the admin must not be able to set a prompt that removes clothing or sexualises the shopper; store listing must not suggest it. [P4, P5, A3, V1] Flag: clear.
3. Use the system photo picker on Android (no READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) and the out-of-process picker on iOS (no full Photos access); camera capture needs a purpose string. [P7, A7, A6] Flag: clear.
4. Before the first upload, show an in-app disclosure that the photo is sent to a third-party AI service to generate the image, and obtain explicit permission; record it; allow withdrawal. [A5, A6, P10, P11] Flag: clear for Apple; interpretation for Play (prominent-disclosure trigger).
5. Account deletion inside the app and at a public web URL (entered in Play Console); deletion must remove uploaded photos and generated images; any retained data disclosed. [P8, A8] Flag: clear.
6. Privacy policy (public URL, in-app link) naming photo collection, the AI vendors or categories receiving photos, retention and deletion; Play Data safety form declaring photo collection and sharing; Apple privacy labels to match. [P9, A6] Flag: clear for Play and guideline 5.1.1; unverified for Apple privacy-label and privacy-manifest specifics (not read).
7. Declare target audience honestly (adults; do not select child age groups) and answer Apple's age-rating questionnaire honestly; store screenshots must suit a 4+ rating. [P13, A9, A10] Flag: clear.
8. Apple 1.2 items beyond reporting (filtering, blocking abusive users, published contact information): provide a contact channel and an ability to suspend abusive accounts. [A1, A2] Flag: interpretation.
9. Child Safety Standards self-certification is not required for a shopping app with no social, chat or dating features. [P12] Flag: clear, provided no such features are added.
10. Server-driven screens must only arrange components already in the binary and must not download executable code. [A11] Flag: interpretation.
11. Let shoppers browse without login if account features are not significant. [A8] Flag: interpretation.

Admin controls and backend
12. Keep OpenRouter prompt logging and "Use of Inputs/Outputs" off; treat these as locked settings. [O1, O2] Flag: clear.
13. Restrict the admin's model list to endpoints whose data policy has been checked; send `provider.zdr: true` or at least `data_collection: "deny"`; decide knowingly whether Safety Retention endpoints are allowed.
