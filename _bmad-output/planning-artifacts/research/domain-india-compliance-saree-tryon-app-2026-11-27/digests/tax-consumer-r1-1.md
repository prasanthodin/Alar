## Obligations the product and admin would have to support

GST rate and classification
- Tax rate must be a per-SKU attribute driven by an HSN code and a product type, not one store-wide rate. (C1, C7, C8) - clear.
- Unstitched saree (plain or embroidered, with or without a running blouse piece): fabric heading by fibre (5007 / 5208-5212 / 5407-5408 and similar), 5% at any price. (C2, C5, C6, C7, C9) - unsettled at source; needs accountant. This is the better-supported reading but rests on a 2017 FAQ extract and blogs; the 2025 notification was not read.
- Ready-to-wear / pre-stitched saree, stitched blouse, and later clothing: Chapter 61/62, 5% when the pre-GST sale value per piece is up to Rs 2,500, 18% above. The cart must compute the band per piece on the discounted pre-tax price, so a coupon can change the rate. (C1, C8, C10) - clear for garments; unsettled for pre-stitched sarees; needs accountant.
- Saree sold with a stitched blouse: support two invoice lines with separate HSN, price and rate. (C9) - needs accountant (composite vs mixed supply not researched).
- Catalogue needs a fibre-composition field (silk, cotton, man-made, blend, zari) because the HSN follows the fibre. (C6, C7) - clear.
- Rate tables must be editable with effective dates; the notification has already been amended at least once. (C11) - clear.

GST compliance
- GST registration before the first inter-state sale; GSTIN shown on invoices. (C13) - needs accountant.
- No TCS under section 52 on own-website sales. (C12) - clear (two secondary sources plus FAQ copy); confirm with accountant.
- Tax invoice per order with HSN (4 digits below Rs 5 crore turnover, 6 above), place of supply, and CGST+SGST for intra-state or IGST for inter-state. (C16, C17) - needs accountant: invoice field list and place-of-supply text not verified.
- E-invoicing (IRN) not required for B2C sales; would apply to any B2B invoices once turnover exceeds Rs 5 crore. (C14) - clear for now; watch for B2C extension.
- Dynamic QR on B2C invoices only above Rs 500 crore turnover. (C15) - clear; not applicable at launch.
- Credit note per return / cancelled order linked to the original invoice; GST on shipping and COD fees. (C17) - needs accountant: nothing established in this run.

Consumer protection (E-Commerce Rules 2020 and 2026 amendment)
- Site and app must display legal name, principal address, website, contact details, and the grievance officer's name, designation and contact. (C19, C22) - clear.
- Grievance workflow: ticket number, acknowledgement within 48 hours, resolution within one month, with admin tracking of both clocks. (C19) - clear.
- Per product: country of origin; for imported goods also importer details; price inclusive of all charges; return, refund, exchange, delivery and payment terms. (C19, C22, C26) - clear.
- Published return/refund/cancellation policy; no cancellation charge on the consumer unless the seller bears the same when it cancels. (C20) - needs lawyer for exact wording.
- From 1 Jan 2027: yearly dark-pattern self-audit and a displayed compliance certificate; "prior price" shown behind any discount claim (needs price-history storage); labelling of any sponsored placement; NCH convergence participation. (C22) - clear on existence; needs lawyer for clause detail and certificate form.
- No dark patterns: no pre-ticked add-ons or auto-added items (basket sneaking), no fake stock or timer pressure (false urgency), full price including shipping and COD fee shown before checkout (drip pricing), no forced app install or sign-up for unrelated things (forced action). (C23, C24) - clear in principle; the 13 definitions were not read at source.
- Entity form: check whether the operating entity must be a company. (C21) - needs lawyer.

Legal metrology
- Each garment listing and the physical tag must carry: manufacturer / marketer / brand owner / importer name and address (plus country of origin if imported), consumer-care email and phone, size or dimensions in metric units (saree length and width in m, blouse piece length), MRP. (C26, C27) - clear for loose garments; needs lawyer on whether a saree shipped in a sealed pack counts as "loose or open" or as a pre-packaged commodity needing all declarations.
- If imported products are ever sold: a country-of-origin filter in search. (C28) - unsettled (single source).

Advertising and AI imagery
- Product photos and descriptions must match the delivered item; disclaimers cannot fix a misleading image and must be in the same language and font as the claim. (C29) - clear.
- AI try-on images should be labelled as AI-generated, kept visually separate from real product photos, and should not be the only depiction of colour or design; mismatch should be an accepted return ground. (C29, C30, C32, C20) - needs lawyer (inference; ASCI rule is self-regulatory and starts 1 Jan 2027).

Payments and COD
- Never store card numbers; card handling stays with Razorpay. (C34) - clear.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method unless the payer asks for another mode of their own; admin must support source refunds via Razorpay and a recorded payer instruction for alternatives. (C33) - clear.
- COD refunds have no "source" to return to: collect payee bank / UPI details and record consent. (C33, C36) - needs lawyer / accountant for process.
- Cap COD order value well under Rs 2 lakh per customer per day / per transaction. (C38) - clear.
- Separate reconciliation for COD remittances from the courier (outside Razorpay settlement). (C36) - clear.
- Failed-payment handling: show "amount will auto-reverse" messaging; the T+5 reversal duty is on banks, but the order system must not ship or double-charge on late confirmations. (C35, C37) - clear.

Returns
- A "no return" label cannot be applied to defective, wrong, late or not-as-described items. Worn / washed exclusions and COD-refusal (RTO) fees are policy choices to be disclosed up front. (C39, C40) - needs lawyer.

Textile claims
- Catalogue terms "silk", "pure silk", "handloom", "Silk Mark", "Handloom Mark", GI names (for example Kanchipuram, Banarasi) should be gated in admin behind evidence fields (certificate number, supplier declaration). (C41, C42) - needs lawyer; the governing texts were not retrieved.

## Glossary

- HSN: Harmonised System of Nomenclature code that classifies goods and determines the GST rate; 4 or 6 digits on invoices depending on turnover.
- CGST / SGST / IGST: central and state GST charged together on intra-state supplies; integrated GST charged on inter-state supplies. Notification rates are stated as the CGST half (2.5% = 5% total).
- Place of supply: the location that decides whether a sale is intra-state or inter-state; for shipped goods, where the movement ends (to be confirmed by the accountant).
- Sale value per piece: the pre-GST consideration for one garment, used for the Rs 2,500 test.
- Schedule I / Schedule II: the 5% and 18% lists in Notification 9/2025-Central Tax (Rate).
- TCS (section 52): tax collected at source by a marketplace operator from its sellers; not the same as income-tax TCS.
- E-invoice / IRN: invoice registered on the government portal with an Invoice Reference Number; B2B only at present.
- Dynamic QR: payment QR on B2C invoices required of very large suppliers.
- Credit note: GST document that reverses all or part of an invoice on return or cancellation.
- Composite supply / mixed supply: bundled supplies taxed at the principal item's rate or the highest rate respectively.
- Inventory e-commerce entity: an e-commerce entity that owns the goods and sells them directly to consumers.
- Marketplace e-commerce entity: a platform connecting third-party sellers and buyers.
- Grievance officer: named person who must acknowledge complaints in 48 hours and resolve in one month.
- CCPA: Central Consumer Protection Authority (India), not the California privacy law.
- Dark pattern: interface design that deceives or pressures a consumer; 13 types specified in the 2023 Guidelines.
- Basket sneaking / false urgency / drip pricing / forced action: adding items without consent / fake scarcity or deadlines / revealing charges late / forcing an unrelated action to proceed.
- MRP: maximum retail price inclusive of all taxes.
- LMPC Rules: Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011.
- Country of origin: where the goods were made; mandatory display online.
- Payment aggregator (PA): RBI-regulated entity (Razorpay) that collects payments for merchants through an escrow account.
- Tokenisation / card-on-file: replacing a card number with a token held by networks and issuers; merchants do not hold card credentials.
- TAT: RBI turnaround time for reversing failed transactions (T+1 or T+5 days, Rs 100 per day after).
- COD: cash on delivery; cash collected by the courier and remitted to the seller.
- RTO: return to origin; a shipment sent back because it was undelivered or refused.
- Section 269ST: income-tax ban on receiving Rs 2 lakh or more in cash.
- Silk Mark / Handloom Mark / GI tag: certification marks and geographical indications; use requires authorisation.
- ASCI: Advertising Standards Council of India, a self-regulatory body.
- SGI: synthetically generated information, the IT Rules term for AI-made or AI-altered media.

## Contradictions

- Saree GST: fabric at 5% at any price (C6, C7; consistent with C2) versus the made-up / apparel treatment with the Rs 2,500 threshold reported by the round-1 run. In this run no source asserted the threshold reading for an unstitched saree; it survives only for pre-stitched sarees (C8). The official 2025 notification and FAQ are silent on sarees as far as read (C5).
- Pre-stitched saree: garment under Chapter 62 (majority) versus "still a saree at 5%" (one guide, unnamed circular) (C8).
- One blog claims the CBIC FAQ still shows the old Rs 1,000 bands; the Sept 2025 FAQ read here does not mention the bands at all (C5).
- Section 52 TCS rate: 1% in older sources, 0.5% from 10 Jul 2024 in newer ones (C12); not relevant to an own-website seller.
- Cancellation charges: "no cancellation charges" versus "not exceeding actual loss" in different summaries (C20).
- Section 24 clause numbers for compulsory registration differ between sources (C13).
