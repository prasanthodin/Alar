## Evidence per criterion

### (A) Medusa + Payload — two systems, both TypeScript
- **SDUI content modelling (25):** Payload's Blocks field is a direct fit for an ordered list of typed, nestable blocks, served over REST and GraphQL (C15). Medusa itself has no CMS; the official guide syncs product data into Payload (C22). Evidence favours this pairing on this criterion, level with (B) and (D), because the CMS is the same.
- **Commerce out of the box (25):** Per-variant inventory with locations and reservations (C11), returns/RMA flow (C14), promotions module, admin dashboard referenced throughout the docs. `manage_inventory` is off by default and must be enabled per variant (C11). Variant-level images were **not verified** in this run. Evidence favours.
- **Speed to launch (20):** Official Payload integration tutorial exists (C22). Razorpay and Shiprocket both have community Medusa v2 packages, but none is official, the Razorpay ones are 6–12 months old, and the Shiprocket ones are at 0.0.x (C20, C21); plan to review or fork.
