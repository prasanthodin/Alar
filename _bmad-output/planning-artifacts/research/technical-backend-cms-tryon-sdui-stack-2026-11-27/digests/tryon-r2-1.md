# Digest tryon-r2-1: source verification of the try-on shortlist

Accessed 2026-11-27. 18 tool calls (curl only). All prices in USD.

**How to read this.** "Read at source: yes" means the provider's own page (or its own GitHub or Hugging Face repo) was fetched and read in this run. "Reseller" means the figure was read on fal.ai, which hosts the model, not on the maker's page. Two providers could not be read at all: ai.google.dev was refused by the network proxy, and kling.ai pages are rendered by JavaScript and returned no text.

Garment-type statements read at source (task F):

- FASHN v1.6: categories are `auto`, `tops`, `bottoms`, `one-pieces`. Full-body on-model garment photos default to a full outfit swap. A `PoseError` is returned when no body pose is detected. No mention of sarees or draped garments.
- FASHN Try-On Max: "supports clothing, shoes, hats, jewelry, bags, and other wearable fashion items", with an optional prompt to adjust how the product is worn. No mention of sarees.
- OpenAI, Qwen: no garment statements; both are general editors. The Qwen card claims improved character consistency for portrait edits.
- No provider page read in this run lists sarees or draped garments as supported or unsupported.
