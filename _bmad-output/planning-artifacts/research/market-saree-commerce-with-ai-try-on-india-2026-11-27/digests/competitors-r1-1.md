## Contradictions

- **Myntra "Try it on Me" status.** YourStory (July 2026, https://yourstory.com/2026/07/myntra-ai-seller-onboarding-shopping-tools) reports it as currently rolling out on the shopper's own photograph. Elle India (https://elle.in/trending/myntra-wants-ai-to-be-your-stylist-12256041, date not seen) describes "Try On Me" as a forthcoming capability showing outfits on the shopper "as a digital avatar". Unresolved: live vs upcoming, and real photo vs avatar. Neither page was read in full.
- **Does Google's India try-on cover sarees?** AI Vastra (vendor) says no; launch press does not mention sarees at all. No Google primary source retrieved. Treat as unresolved, leaning "not covered".
- **Sai Silks trailing revenue.** One aggregator shows about INR 16.33 billion TTM, another about INR 15.74 billion (stockanalysis.com vs other aggregator in the same result set). Use company filings.
- **Meesho fashion share.** One blog gives 21-23% GMV share in fashion; GrabOn gives about 18% of online fashion. Both are low-quality and may use different denominators.

## Leads

1. Myntra "Try it on Me": confirm current availability, whether sarees/ethnic are in scope, and user reviews (Play Store reviews, Myntra newsroom, Reddit). This is the single most decision-relevant unknown.
2. Google try-on in India: read Google India blog/help page for the category list and whether sarees/draped garments are excluded.
3. Meesho saree try-on: did the 2024 Google Cloud pipeline ship to shoppers, and in what form? Check Meesho tech blog and the Meesho IPO industry report (https://investor.meesho.com/investor-web/_next/docs/ipo/industry-report.pdf) for saree/ethnic category data.
4. Vendor pricing and real quality: Perfect Corp sari API, TryOnCloud, TryVastra, AI Vastra, Genlook. Pricing pages were unreachable; a hands-on test on 5-10 sarees (Nivi and regional drapes, heavy borders/pallu) would beat any press claim.
5. Third-party overlay apps (Changing Room, Looksy AI, MirrorX, FitMe): install counts and review ratings as a demand signal and as a "try-on is already a free commodity" risk.
6. Traction for unverified players: Taneira (Titan annual report), Nalli, Pothys, Kalyan Silks, Mirraw, Utsav Fashion, Peachmode, Koskii, Ajio, Nykaa Fashion, Tata Cliq; Sai Silks online share (Q2/Q3 FY26 call transcripts on nsearchives.nseindia.com).
7. Imagery practices (question 4) were not researched from primary product pages; needs a direct audit of product pages on Myntra, Meesho, Taneira, Suta, Karagiri, Kalamandir.
8. Hosts to allowlist for a follow-up round: yourstory.com, business-standard.com, tryoncloud.com, yce.perfectcorp.com.
