## Contradictions

- **Myntra status wording.** YourStory (July 2026) says "currently being rolled out"; Deccan Herald (same period) says "piloting". Both describe a limited, not general, release. Neither names categories. The round-1 Elle contradiction could not be located.
- **Round-1 date for Meesho.** Round 1 said July 2024; the Google Cloud blog post is dated 7 August 2024 per the search extract.
- **Perfect Corp and sarees.** Round 1 listed Perfect Corp as claiming saree try-on. Nothing retrieved in this run shows a saree or ethnic-wear claim by Perfect Corp; its press describes generic clothes try-on and accessories.
- **Vendor pricing.** TryVastra: INR 20 per credit (own site) vs INR 599 a month for 25 images (CatalogX). TryOnCloud: its own pages give conflicting plan prices.
- **Vendor ratings.** TryVastra shows 4.9 from 10 reviews on-page and 4.8 from 1,024 reviews in structured data.
- **Myntra image specs.** Vendor guides disagree on minimum resolution and on whether PNG is accepted. Myntra's own seller document was not retrieved.
