## Contradictions

- **Does the Rs 2,500 GST threshold apply to sarees at all?** Decision-critical and unresolved. One secondary source (among the results for the CBIC notification query; https://www.garmentshopmanager.com/learn/gst-rate-on-garments.html or https://taxgarden.in/blog/gst-on-textiles-garments-india-2026-rates-hsn-codes-guide, the extract did not attribute it precisely) says sarees are classified as fabric, not garments, so the threshold does not apply and they stay at 5%. Another lists sarees under the Chapter 62 apparel line with the 5%/18% split. A third (https://www.techbloat.com/gst-rates-on-clothes-in-india-2025-rate-change-old-cbic-bands-and-faqs.html) says not to assume a special rate for sarees and notes CBIC FAQs still show the old Rs 1,000 bands. The notification's Schedule entries were not read. Plausibly the answer depends on the product: an unstitched saree vs one sold with a stitched blouse or as a ready-to-wear/pre-draped saree. This is my inference, not a retrieved finding.
- **COD RTO rate.** Meesho prospectus: 22.3% of COD orders undelivered (FY25). ClickPost network: 37.51% of COD orders RTO (FY2026); fashion band 21-32%. Razorpay: 25-30%. DFU: 58% at festive peak. These measure different populations (a marketplace with its own logistics vs D2C brands on a vendor network vs peak season) and are not directly comparable.
- **Saraswati Saree Depot gross margin.** Moneyworks4me gives 6.3% for FY25 (https://www.moneyworks4me.com/check-before-you-invest/ssdl); StockAnalysis shows 9.5-12.9% (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/SSDL/financials/income-statement/). Different cost definitions likely; neither is the company's own figure.
- **Vedant Fashions gross margin.** Company-reported about 67.2% (FY25) vs about 73% on StockAnalysis for an earlier year (https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/MANYAVAR/financials); definitional difference.
- **Saree market size.** Redseer about $12 bn vs IMARC USD 5.76 bn (2024) / 6.15 bn (2025) (https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-saree-market/toc).
- **Forward shipping per 500 g.** Rs 19-70 (ShipPrime) vs Rs 50-90 for Delhivery (Shiprocket blog) vs "from about Rs 20" aggregator entry rate (https://indiapost.org/cheapest-courier-service, a site of unverified independence). Reflects contracted vs retail rates and zones.
