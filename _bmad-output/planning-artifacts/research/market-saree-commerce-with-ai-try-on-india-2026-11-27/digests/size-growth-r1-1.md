## Contradictions

- **Total saree market size differs by up to about 2x.** Technopak-linked projection: about Rs 61,553-61,700 crore for FY25 (https://apparelresources.com/business-news/retail/saree-market-touch-rs-61700-crore-fy-25/, https://www.indiaretailing.com/?p=110020). Secondary 2025-26 articles: Rs 70,000 crore in FY24 and Rs 80,000 crore (https://upstox.com/news/upstox-originals/investing/saree-vs-fast-fashion-how-india-is-splitting-its-fashion-wallet/article-181378). Trade officials: over Rs 1 lakh crore (https://textilevaluechain.in/?p=94861). IMARC: USD 5.76 bn for 2024, about Rs 48,000 crore at roughly Rs 83-84/USD (my conversion) (https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-saree-market/toc). Range: about Rs 48,000 crore to over Rs 1,00,000 crore. Growth is more consistent: 5-7% a year across Technopak and IMARC.
- **Online share of sarees.** IMARC's "offline 65%" implies about 35% non-offline (https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-saree-market/toc), against about 8% online penetration of all retail in CY2025 per Redseer (https://www.deccanherald.com/business/indias-online-retail-market-to-cross-90-bn-in-cy26-redseer-4149814) and 10-12% for fashion (https://bain.com/insights/indias-digital-fashion-disruptors, https://www.grabon.in/indulge/fashion/online-fashion-statistics-india/). These cannot both describe the same market; the IMARC split is the weaker source.
- **South India share.** Technopak: South is about 50% of the ORGANISED saree market (Fibre2Fashion URL above). Shiprocket restates it as about 50% of India's saree market overall (https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/export-saree-industry-trends/). The scope differs; the organised-only reading is the sourced one.
- **Organised share.** "Unorganised over 60%" (implying organised under 40%) vs "organised 15-25%" (DFU / secondary sources above).
- **Online fashion size 2025.** USD 21.59 bn (Credence) vs USD 25.6 bn (ECDB); older Bain figure USD 11 bn (undated).
