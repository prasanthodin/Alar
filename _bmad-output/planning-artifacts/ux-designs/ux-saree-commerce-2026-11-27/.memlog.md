- (event) Create intent, Fast path (founder asked for speed). Stakes: consumer, public launch. Form factor: website (Next.js, Tailwind CSS 4, shadcn) and Android app (Expo / React Native) sharing tokens; iOS later.
- (decision by user) Primary colour from the lavender family with a distinctive hue; deep lavender for buttons and text, light lavender tints for surfaces and accents.
- (decision by user) Zero shadows anywhere, web and app; depth by tinted surfaces and fine borders.
- (decision by user) Glass effects only on navigation bars and floating controls; never over saree photos or as page backgrounds.
- (decision by user) Carousels are swiped by the Shopper; no auto-rotation.
- (decision by user) Consistent, right-sized type scale and exact spacing (margins, padding, gaps) for mobile and web; micro-animations welcome; modern, distinctive, attractive.
- (decision by user) Front end: shadcn + Tailwind + TypeScript on the web; images, video and carousels may come from Higgsfield; follow current iPhone and Android design direction.
- (assumption) Three lavender directions offered for the founder to pick: A Wisteria (hue about 295, blue-violet), B Heather (hue about 312, mauve), C Iris (hue about 280, indigo-leaning). DESIGN.md is drafted on A until the founder chooses.
- (assumption) Product photos always sit on a neutral, untinted surface so the interface colour does not shift how a saree's colour is read.
- (event) Sources: PRD (final), architecture spine (final), UX research digest research/ux-direction-2026-11-27/digest.md, docs/idea-notes.md, docs/design-references/, installed design skills (animate, animate-expo, apple-design, mobile-native, gestures).
- (event) Colour-themes page rendered (.working/color-themes-1.html) and published as an artifact: three lavender directions A Wisteria, B Heather, C Iris on the same product card, with shared type, spacing, corner, depth and motion scales. Awaiting the founder's pick.
