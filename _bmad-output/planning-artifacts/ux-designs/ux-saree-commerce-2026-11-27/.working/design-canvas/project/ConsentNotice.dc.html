What we takeOne photo of you that you choose, and the photo of the saree you want to try.
WhyOnly to make a picture of you wearing that saree. We do not use it for ads, for training AI, or for anything else.
Who receives itOur AI service providers: Google (Gemini image) and OpenAI (GPT Image), reached through OpenRouter. They process the photo for us and may do so outside India. They are not allowed to keep it or train on it.
How long we keep itYour photo: until you delete it, or 30 days after you last used try-on. Try-on pictures: until you delete them.
Who can use try-onOnly adults, 18 or older, and only with a photo of yourself. Photos of children or of other people are refused.
Your choicesYou can see, correct or delete your photo and pictures, and withdraw this consent at any time in Profile, Privacy and consent. Withdrawing deletes them at once and does not affect shopping.
If something is wrongWrite to our Grievance Officer from Profile, Help. We reply within 48 hours. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
