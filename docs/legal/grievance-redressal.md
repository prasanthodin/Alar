## 3. What you can complain about

Anything to do with {{SHOP_NAME}}, including:

- an order, payment, delivery, return or refund;
- a product that is not as shown or described;
- a Try-on result, or how your photo was handled;
- your personal data, or a privacy request that was not answered;
- a photo or Try-on result that shows you and that you did not agree to;
- anything on the website or app you believe is misleading or unfair.

## 4. How to complain

4.1 Use any of these:

- the complaint option in your account or on the order;
- email to {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}};
- phone to {{GRIEVANCE_PHONE}};
- a letter to the address above.

4.2 Tell us:

- your name and a mobile number or email where we can reach you;
- your Order number, if the complaint is about an order;
- what happened and what you would like us to do;
- photos or screenshots, if they help.

4.3 You do not need an account to complain. If you are complaining about a photo or Try-on result that shows you, describe the image as best you can. We do not ask for proof beyond a reasonable description.

## 5. What happens next

5.1 **Ticket number.** Every complaint gets a ticket number. Quote it when you follow up.

5.2 **Acknowledgement within 48 hours.** We confirm that we have received your complaint, with the ticket number, within 48 hours.

5.3 **Resolution within one month.** We resolve your complaint within one month of receiving it and tell you the outcome and our reasons.

5.4 We may ask you for more information. We may also need to confirm your identity before giving out or changing personal data.

5.5 We message you when the complaint is acknowledged and when it is resolved.

5.6 Complaining never affects your orders, your account or your right to a refund.

## 6. If you are not satisfied

You do not have to accept our answer. These routes are open to you.

6.1 **Ask us to look again.** Reply on the same ticket and say why you disagree.

6.2 **National Consumer Helpline.** For complaints about a purchase, delivery, return, refund or unfair practice, you can contact the National Consumer Helpline run by the Government of India's Department of Consumer Affairs: {{NCH_CONTACT}}.

6.3 **Consumer forums.** You may take a consumer complaint to any consumer forum or authority that the law allows you to approach.

6.4 **Data Protection Board of India.** For complaints about your personal data, including your Shopper photo, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is functioning.

6.5 **Payment problems.** For a payment that was debited and not refunded as this policy says, you can also raise it with your bank or UPI app.
