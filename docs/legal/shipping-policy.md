# {{SHOP_NAME}} Shipping Policy

> **Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy is part of our [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.md). An **Order** is a paid purchase.

## 1. Where we deliver

1.1 We deliver to addresses in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Puducherry.

1.2 We do not deliver elsewhere in India or abroad at present.

1.3 Within these places, a few pincodes may not be served by our shipping service. The pincode check tells you.

## 2. Pincode check

2.1 Enter your pincode on a Design page or in your cart to see whether we deliver there.

2.2 If we do not deliver to your pincode, we tell you before checkout. You cannot pay for an Order we cannot deliver.

## 3. Estimated delivery dates

3.1 The pincode check shows an earliest and a latest delivery date for your address.

3.2 These dates are estimates. Weather, holidays, strikes and other events outside our control can cause delay.

3.3 If your Order is running later than the latest date shown, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. If it has not been dispatched, you can cancel it for a full refund.

## 4. Shipping charges

4.1 Any shipping charge is shown before you begin checkout.

4.2 The total you see before you pay is the total you are charged. We add nothing during checkout.

## 5. Tracking your Order

5.1 We message you when your Order is confirmed, dispatched, out for delivery and delivered, by SMS and by email if you gave one.

5.2 Your order history shows the current stage: Paid, Packed, Dispatched, Out for delivery, Delivered.

## 6. Receiving your parcel

6.1 Please make sure someone can receive the parcel at the delivery address, and that the phone number on the Order can be reached.

6.2 Check your address and pincode before you pay. To correct an address after paying, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} at once. We can change it only before dispatch.

## 7. If a delivery fails

7.1 If the shipping service cannot deliver, for example because no one was available or the address could not be found, your Order shows "Delivery failed" and tells you what happens next. We also message you.

7.2 The shipping service may try again. How many times depends on the shipping service.

7.3 If the Order still cannot be delivered, the parcel comes back to us and the Order shows "Returned to shop". We then refund the full amount you paid, to the payment method you used, started within 2 working days of the parcel reaching us.

## 8. Damaged or opened parcels

8.1 If the parcel is visibly damaged, torn or opened when it arrives, you may refuse it. Tell us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will treat it as an undeliverable Order and refund you.

8.2 If you accept the parcel and find the saree damaged, or the wrong saree, or something missing, request a Return with the reason "defective or wrong item" within {{RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS}} days of delivery. Return shipping is free and you get a full refund. See the [Returns, Refunds and Cancellation Policy](returns-refunds-cancellation-policy.md).

8.3 Photos of the parcel and the saree help us resolve this quickly and take it up with the shipping service. Please tell us as soon as you can.

## 9. Lost parcels

If tracking shows no movement and the latest estimated date has passed, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. If the shipping service confirms the parcel is lost, we refund the full amount you paid.

## 10. Help

Questions: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Complaints: see [Grievance Redressal](grievance-redressal.md).
