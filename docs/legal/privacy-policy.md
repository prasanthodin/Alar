# {{SHOP_NAME}} Privacy Policy

> **Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy explains what personal data {{SHOP_NAME}} collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It covers the website and the Android app.

Words we use: **Try-on** is the feature that makes an image of you wearing a saree. A **Shopper photo** is the photo of yourself you upload for Try-on. A **Try-on result** is the AI-generated image Try-on returns. **Try-on consent** is your recorded acceptance of the [Try-on Consent Notice](try-on-consent-notice.md).

## 2. The short version

- You can browse without giving us any personal details.
- We collect what we need to sign you in, take payment, deliver your order, handle returns and answer you.
- Your Shopper photo is used only for Try-on, only with your separate consent, and is sent to outside AI services that may be outside India.
- We never use Shopper photos or Try-on results for advertising, analytics or training AI models.
- We do not sell your data. We do not send marketing messages unless you separately agree.
- You can withdraw Try-on consent, delete a photo, or delete your account at any time.

## 4. Our basis for using your data

4.1 **Consent.** We use your Shopper photo and Try-on results only with your Try-on consent. That consent is asked for on its own screen, is never pre-ticked, and is not bundled with these terms. You can withdraw it at any time (section 9).

4.2 **Data you give us for a stated purpose, and legal duties.** For signing in, buying, delivery, returns and complaints, we use the details you give us for the purpose you gave them. We also keep some records because tax, consumer and payment rules require it. We treat these as "legitimate uses" under the DPDP Act.

4.3 **Usage counts and security.** We count how the shop is used and keep security records. These counts are not tied to advertising profiles and are not shared with advertisers. We do not track you across other websites or apps.

4.4 **Marketing.** We send marketing messages only if you separately agree. You can stop them at any time.

4.5 Most duties under the DPDP Act and its Rules are expected to start around May 2027. We follow this policy from the effective date above in any case.

| Our accountant and legal advisers | Invoices, order records and complaint records as needed | Tax filings and legal advice |

**About the AI services.** By default your Shopper photo goes to OpenRouter, a service that passes requests to AI model providers, and then to the model provider that makes the image. The services that can receive your photo are listed above and in the Try-on Consent Notice. If we want to add a service that is not listed, we publish a new notice and ask for your consent again before your next Try-on.

For these services:

- we choose only models that do not keep Shopper photos to train their models;
- we keep request logging at the AI service switched off;
- where a service offers a no-retention option, we use it;
- we record which service received each request, so that we can tell you and can ask it to delete.

These services work under their own published terms. An AI service may keep content that its safety systems flag as child sexual abuse material, as its terms and the law require.

We stay responsible to you for what these services do with your data on our behalf.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## 6. Transfers outside India

6.1 The AI services, and the model providers they route to, may process your Shopper photo on computers outside India. This is the main way your data leaves India.

6.2 Some of our other service providers (for example hosting, email or messaging) may also store or process data outside India.

6.3 We will not send personal data to any country that the Government of India restricts for this purpose.

6.4 If you do not want your photo processed outside India, do not use Try-on. Everything else in the shop works without it.

## 7. How long we keep your data

| Data | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| Shopper photos and Try-on results | Deleted when not used for 30 days; or at once when you delete them, withdraw Try-on consent, or delete your account |
| Try-on results that fail our automatic check | Never stored |

| Ratings and comments | Until you delete your account; after that only without your personal details |
| Device and usage information | Only as long as needed for security and for counting use of the shop |
| Backups | Deleted data drops out of backups as they are replaced on their normal cycle |

When you withdraw Try-on consent or delete your account, we finish deleting your photos and results from our systems within 24 hours, and within the same time we send a deletion request to each AI service that received them. We record the outcome of each request and follow up on any that fail or go unanswered.

## 8. How we protect your data

- Personal data and Shopper photos are encrypted when stored and when sent.
- Shopper photos can be reached only through short-lived links.
- Access to our back office needs a password and a second factor.
- Every access to personal data is logged, and the logs are kept for one year.
- We keep backups in a separate location.
- We never store card numbers.

No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong, section 12 applies.
