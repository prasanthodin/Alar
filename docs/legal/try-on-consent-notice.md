## Part B: Full version ("Read more")

### 1. Who is asking

{{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}}, {{REGISTERED_ADDRESS}}, which runs {{SHOP_NAME}}.

### 2. What data

- The full-length photo of yourself that you choose or take for Try-on (your "Shopper photo"). We receive only the photo you pick, not your gallery.
- The Try-on results: AI-generated images of you wearing our sarees, made from that photo.
- A record of each request: which saree, which AI service, when, and whether it worked. This record does not contain the photo.
- A record of this consent: the time, the notice version and your two confirmations.

### 3. What for

Only to make Try-on results for you and to keep your photo and results so you can use them again.

We do not use your photo or results for advertising, analytics or training AI models. We do not show them to other shoppers. We never use a Try-on result as a product photo.

### 4. Who receives your photo

These AI services: {{AI_SERVICE_LIST}}.

By default your photo goes to OpenRouter, a service that passes requests on to AI model providers, and then to the model provider that makes the image. They receive your photo, a picture of the saree and instructions for the image. They do not receive your name, phone number or address.

We choose only models that do not keep your photo for training. We keep request logging at the AI service switched off, and use a no-retention option where a service offers one. An AI service may keep content that its safety systems flag as child sexual abuse material.

If we want to send photos to a service not listed here, we will show you a new notice and ask again first.

Your photo and results are also stored, encrypted, with our hosting service.

### 5. Where

The AI services may process your photo on computers outside India.

### 6. How long

- We delete your Shopper photo and Try-on results when you have not used them for 30 days.
- We delete them at once when you delete them yourself, withdraw this consent, or delete your account. Deletion on our side is complete within 24 hours, and within the same time we ask each AI service that received them to delete them.
- A result that fails our automatic check is never stored.
- Access logs, which never contain the photo, are kept for one year. The record of your consent and of each request (no photo) is kept so we can show what you agreed to and that deletion was done.

### 7. What you confirm

- You are 18 or older. Try-on is for adults only.
- The photo is of you, and only you. Uploading a photo of anyone else is not allowed.

### 8. What you will see

Every Try-on result is labelled "AI-generated". It is an illustration, not a photograph, and not a promise of how the saree will look on you. For true colour and design, use the product photos and the full saree view.

### 9. How to withdraw

Go to Account > Try-on consent and tap "Withdraw consent". It takes one tap and a confirmation, the same effort as agreeing. Your photos and results are deleted, and Try-on will ask for consent again next time. You can also delete a single photo or result without withdrawing. Withdrawing does not affect your orders.

### 10. Your other rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, which AI services received your photos, and for correction or deletion. Use the privacy request option in your account or write to {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

### 11. How to complain

Contact our Grievance Officer: {{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER_NAME}}, {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}, {{GRIEVANCE_PHONE}}. You get a ticket number, an acknowledgement within 48 hours and a resolution within one month.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is functioning.

### 12. If you say no

Try-on will not be available. Everything else, including browsing, the full saree view and buying, works as usual.
