# {{SHOP_NAME}} Returns, Refunds and Cancellation Policy

> **Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy is part of our [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.md). An **Order** is a paid purchase. A **Return** is your request to send an item back for a refund.

## 1. In short

- Cancel any time before dispatch for a full refund.
- Return within {{RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS}} days of delivery.
- Not as shown, defective, or wrong item: always accepted, return shipping free, full refund.
- Any other reason: accepted if the saree is unworn, unwashed and has its tags.
- Refunds go to the payment method you used. We start them within 2 working days.

## 2. Cancelling an Order

2.1 You can cancel an Order from your order history at any time before it is dispatched. We refund the full amount you paid, including any shipping charge.

2.2 Once an Order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled. You can return it after delivery under this policy.

2.3 We may cancel an Order before dispatch if we cannot fulfil it, for example if a Piece is found damaged while packing or we cannot deliver to your address. We tell you why and refund you in full.

## 3. The return window

3.1 You can request a Return within {{RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS}} days of the day your Order is delivered.

3.2 The return window and conditions are also shown on each Design page and at checkout.

## 4. Reasons we always accept

4.1 We always accept a Return made within the return window if:

- (a) **Not as shown.** The saree is noticeably different from its product photos, full saree view or description, for example in colour, design, fibre or size.
- (b) **Defective.** The saree has a tear, hole, stain, weaving fault or other damage.
- (c) **Wrong item.** You received a different saree from the one you ordered.

4.2 For these reasons, return shipping is free and we refund the full amount you paid, including any shipping charge.

4.3 A saree that looks different from its Try-on result but matches its product photos and description is not "not as shown". Try-on results are AI-generated illustrations. You can still return it under section 5.

4.4 Small differences that are normal for hand-woven sarees, and slight colour differences between screens, are not defects (see clause 4 of the Terms of Use). If you are unsure, ask for the Return and tell us what you see.

4.5 Tell us what is wrong when you request the Return. Photos help us act faster.

## 5. Returns for any other reason

5.1 If you have changed your mind or have any other reason, we accept a Return made within the return window if the saree is:

- (a) unworn and unused;
- (b) unwashed, not dry-cleaned and not ironed;
- (c) unaltered: no fall or edging stitched on, and the blouse piece not cut or stitched;
- (d) free of stains, marks, perfume and smells;
- (e) with its tags attached and in its original packing as far as possible.

5.2 Unfolding the saree and draping it briefly to look at it does not count as wearing it.

5.3 For these Returns we refund the price of the saree. We do not refund the original shipping charge, and we may deduct a return shipping charge. Any deduction is shown to you before you confirm the Return request.

## 6. How to raise a Return

6.1 Sign in and open the Order in your order history.

6.2 Choose the item, tap "Request return" and pick a reason.

6.3 Book a pickup. The shipping service collects the parcel from your delivery address.

6.4 Pack the saree with its tags, folded as it came.

6.5 You can follow the Return in your order history. Its stages are: Requested, Pickup booked, Picked up, Received, then Accepted or Rejected, and Refunded.

6.6 If you cannot use the app or website, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your Order number.

## 7. Inspection

7.1 We inspect every returned saree when it reaches us.

7.2 For a Return under section 4, we check that the saree is the one we sent and look at the problem you reported.

7.3 For a Return under section 5, we check the conditions in clause 5.1.

7.4 If the Return passes, it is Accepted and we start your refund.

## 8. Rejected Returns

8.1 We may reject a Return if:

- (a) the saree does not meet the conditions in clause 5.1 and none of the reasons in section 4 applies;
- (b) the item sent back is not the one we sent you; or
- (c) the saree was damaged after delivery.

8.2 If we reject a Return, we tell you why and send you photos from our inspection. We then send the saree back to you.

8.3 If you disagree, complain to our Grievance Officer (see [Grievance Redressal](grievance-redressal.md)). A rejection does not take away any right you have under consumer law.

## 9. Refunds

9.1 Refunds go back to the payment method you used: the same UPI account, card or bank account. We can use a different method only if you ask us to.

9.2 We start a refund within 2 working days of:

- (a) your cancellation;
- (b) a Return being Accepted; or
- (c) an undeliverable Order reaching us (section 10).

9.3 You see the refund amount and the date we started it in your order history, and we message you.

9.4 After we start a refund, how soon the money shows in your account depends on your bank and the payment service.

9.5 We do not offer exchanges at present. To get a different saree, return the first and place a new Order.

9.6 If only part of an Order is returned, we refund that part.

## 10. Orders that cannot be delivered

10.1 If the shipping service cannot deliver your Order and it comes back to us, we refund the full amount you paid.

10.2 See the [Shipping Policy](shipping-policy.md) for what happens when a delivery attempt fails.

## 11. Payments taken without an Order

If your payment is confirmed but we cannot create an Order, for example because the last Piece was sold while you were paying, we refund you automatically and message you within 5 minutes of the confirmation.
